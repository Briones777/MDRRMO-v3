About MDRRMO

The MDRRMO ensures disaster preparedness, response, and recovery operations across the municipality. It protects lives through coordinated emergency systems and community resilience programs.

Through training, rescue operations, and public awareness campaigns, the office strengthens rapid emergency response capacity.

PAGASA Advisory

El Niño Safety Advisory

ENSO-neutral conditions expected to shift to El Niño by mid-2026. Prepare for extended dry spells and elevated heat index levels in Cavite Province.

Safety Bulletin

Fire Prevention Tips

Cooking safety: remain in kitchen while frying or broiling. Do not overload sockets. Keep fire extinguishers accessible in all households.

DRRM Bulletin

Emergency Preparedness

Assemble a 72-hour supply kit (water, food, radio, medicines). Establish a family communication plan. Identify evacuation routes in your barangay.
